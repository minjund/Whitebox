- `status-control.svg`: retains A's three filled panels and mint sidebar;
  adds three circular status marks in the top pane and a geometric terminal
  prompt in the large pane. Intended meaning: see multiple AI tasks and act.
- `connected-control.svg`: replaces the top pane with three connected work
  units and moves the mint accent to the terminal pane. Intended meaning:
  connected AI workspaces and direct intervention.

The added marks use basic geometric paths. No provider logos, brand artwork,
