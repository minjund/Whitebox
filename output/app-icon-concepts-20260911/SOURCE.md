# Whitebox app-icon concepts

Both concepts adapt permissively licensed Lucide artwork. Complete upstream
copyright and license notices accompany them in `LICENSE.txt`; retain that file
when redistributing these icons or modified versions.

Upstream revision: `5d592a96aaeb4a3a09ffd8134f8f5ed878c9a0c5`

## A: Workspace

Source: https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide/blob/5d592a96aaeb4a3a09ffd8134f8f5ed878c9a0c5/icons/panels-top-left.svg

The original three-pane layout is converted from thin outlines to three filled,
separated rounded panels. The sidebar panel is mint green and the tile background
is charcoal. Intended meaning: several AI workspaces in one control room.

## B: Connected workspaces

Source: https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide/blob/5d592a96aaeb4a3a09ffd8134f8f5ed878c9a0c5/icons/columns-3.svg

The original three-column layout is filled, its dividers are opened at the top
and shortened to leave a shared base, and the middle column receives a green
accent. Intended meaning: three agent workspaces connected in one application.

The original SVGs are retained as `lucide-panels-top-left.svg` and
`lucide-columns-3.svg`. These previews contain no AI-generated bitmap, fonts used
as logo glyphs, gradients, textures, or Dropbox artwork. Copyright permission
does not establish trademark clearance or exclusive ownership.
